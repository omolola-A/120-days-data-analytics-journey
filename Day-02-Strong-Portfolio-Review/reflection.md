## Day 2 of my Career Reset challenge.

![](https://github.com/omolola-A/120-days-data-analytics-journey/blob/main/Portfolio%20image.jpeg)


Today’s task was to review two strong data analytics portfolios and try to understand what actually makes a project stand out beyond just charts and code. I went through a hotel booking analysis project built with Python that was clearly documented on GitHub, with a README that linked out to a longer write-up explaining the business story. What stood out immediately was how the analyst started with a clear problem recurring booking cancellations affecting revenue and room usage and then walked through the analysis step by step before drawing insights like price increases, online agent bookings, and long waiting lists contributing to cancellations.

The second project focused on movie genre analysis and followed a similar structure. The objective of the analysis was stated upfront, along with what the analyst intended to achieve by the end. It made the work easier to follow because I wasn’t guessing what the analysis was trying to prove.

From both reviews, I’m starting to see that strong portfolios are less about complex tools and more about clarity: defining the problem, exploring and cleaning the data, generating insights that matter, and making recommendations someone could actually act on.

I didn’t build anything new today, but I feel like I fixed a blind spot in how I evaluate my own projects. Next step is to revisit one of my earlier analyses and check whether someone unfamiliar with my work would clearly understand the problem I was trying to solve.
