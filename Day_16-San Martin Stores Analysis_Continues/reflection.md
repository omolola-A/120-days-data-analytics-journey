## Day 16 – San Martin Stores Analysis (Time Intelligence Challenge)

![](https://github.com/omolola-A/120-days-data-analytics-journey/blob/main/Day16_IMG.png)

Today I continued working on the San Martin Stores project, and this dataset forced me to think differently.

All the orders were placed in January 2021. No previous year. No month-over-month comparison. My usual time intelligence approach didn’t apply.

But I still wanted a time-based comparison.

So I decided to calculate previous week performance instead.

This part stretched me. I had to research, rethink my calendar table, and add additional columns to support week-level analysis. After several attempts and adjustments, I was finally able to write the DAX measures for Revenue Previous Week and related KPIs.

What looked simple at first turned into a deeper modeling exercise.

I’m starting to see that time intelligence isn’t just about functions — it’s about structuring the date table correctly from the beginning.
