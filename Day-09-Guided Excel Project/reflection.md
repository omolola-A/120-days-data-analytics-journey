Today was about turning the cleaned data and KPIs into something decision-ready.

I worked through the analysis layer:

* Revenue monthly trend

* Product category performance

* Customer segment breakdown

Geographical analysis (top revenue-generating state and regional customer composition)

I also referenced the time intelligence KPIs and applied conditional formatting to make the year-over-year changes easier to interpret.

After that, I created a separate sheet for the dashboard, removed gridlines and the formula bar, and imported a wireframe template to structure the layout.

What stood out to me was how much thought goes into presentation. The numbers matter, but how they’re arranged changes how quickly someone understands the story.

Still learning how to balance analysis depth with clean design. Next step is refining the dashboard so it feels less like a spreadsheet and more like a decision tool.
