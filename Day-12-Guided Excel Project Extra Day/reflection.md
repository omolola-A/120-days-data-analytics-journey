## Day 12 – Rebuilding the Interactive Sales Dashboard

Today wasn’t about adding new features. It was about repetition.

I revisited the entire guided Excel project and worked through it again from scratch — cleaning the data, rebuilding the model, recreating the KPIs, and restructuring the dashboard layout.

The goal was simple: test what I actually remember without relying too heavily on step-by-step guidance.

Some parts flowed more naturally this time, especially the KPI calculations and pivot structure. Other parts, like certain formatting decisions and layout alignment, still required slowing down and thinking carefully.

Rebuilding it helped me see the difference between “following along” and truly understanding the workflow.

Next step: try recreating a similar dashboard without any guidance and see where I struggle.
