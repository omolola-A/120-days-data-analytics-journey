## Day 34/120 – Working Through Danny’s Diner Case Study

![](https://github.com/omolola-A/120-days-data-analytics-journey/blob/main/Day-34.png)

Today I worked on the Danny’s Diner SQL case study, focusing on answering real business questions using a small but structured dataset.
The dataset looked simple at first—just three tables (sales, menu, members)—but the challenge was in how they connect and how to extract meaningful insights from them.
I wrote multiple SQL queries to answer questions around customer spending, visit patterns, and product preferences. Most of the work involved joining tables correctly and making sure the logic matched the question being asked.
Some parts that slowed me down:
* Getting the joins right without duplicating records
* Handling questions that required thinking through “before” and “after” membership
* Using ranking functions only where they actually made sense
One thing that stood out is how even a small dataset can require careful thinking. It’s easy to write a query that runs, but harder to make sure it answers the question correctly.
This felt less like practicing SQL syntax and more like thinking through business logic step by step.
