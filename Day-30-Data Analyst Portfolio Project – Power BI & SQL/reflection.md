## Day 30/120 of my Data Analytics Challenge

![](https://github.com/omolola-A/120-days-data-analytics-journey/blob/main/Day_30.jpeg)

Today built directly on the SQL work from yesterday. I started a new guided project: **“Data Analyst Portfolio Project – Power BI & SQL.”**

Instead of jumping straight into queries or dashboards, the first step focused on understanding the **business request behind the report**. I worked through how a request from stakeholders gets translated into something analysts can actually build.

The exercise walked through documenting the request, turning it into a **business demand overview**, and then breaking it down into **user stories**. That part stood out to me because it shows how technical work connects to real business needs.

It’s easy to think analysis starts with data, but today reminded me that it often starts with **clarifying the problem first**.

Next step: cleaning and restructuring the data so it aligns with the user stories and the questions the business is trying to answer.

Still learning how the pieces connect between business questions, SQL work, and the final dashboard. #DataAnalyticsLockedIn
