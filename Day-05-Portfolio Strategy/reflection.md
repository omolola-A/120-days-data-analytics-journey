## Day 5 – Portfolio Strategy: Building a Project Idea Bank

![](https://github.com/omolola-A/120-days-data-analytics-journey/blob/main/Day5-image.jpeg)

Today wasn’t about dashboards or datasets. It was about direction.

I realized that if I keep building random projects, my portfolio will look busy but not intentional. So I paused and created a small “project idea bank” focused on real business problems I could actually solve with data.

**Here are the five I’m starting with:**

* Declining sales performance across products or regions

* Low rate of repeat purchases and high client attrition

* Unclear marketing campaign effectiveness and ROI

* Slow service delivery or order processing times

* Unbalanced inventory leading to excess stock and stockouts

While writing these out, I noticed something: in past projects, I jumped straight into analysis without clearly stating the business problem. That made my work look like exploration instead of decision support.

What I’m figuring out now is that the strength of a project isn’t just in the dashboard — it’s in how clearly the problem is defined before any data is touched.

Next step is to pick one of these and fully scope it out: define stakeholders, metrics, and expected decisions before I open any tool.

If you’re building a portfolio too, how do you decide which problems are worth solving?
