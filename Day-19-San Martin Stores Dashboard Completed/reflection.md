## Day 19 – San Martin Stores Dashboard Completed

![](https://github.com/omolola-A/120-days-data-analytics-journey/blob/main/Day-19_IMG.png)

Today I completed the San Martin Stores analysis dashboard.

I finalized the remaining charts, connected all slicers properly, and made sure the interactions worked across the entire report. What started as a messy multi-table dataset is now a structured model feeding a fully interactive dashboard.

** Key insights: **

* All transactions occurred in January 2021.

* Regional performance: Castilla y León leads with over $10.5M in sales, well ahead of Aragón ($5.9M) and Cataluña ($4.1M).

* Product categories: Carretera (Road bikes) is the top-selling category (~$8.19M), followed by Montaña and Paseo.

* Profitability: Overall profit margin stands at 28.39%, with margins relatively consistent across product categories.

This project pushed me to think more independently — from cleaning seven tables, to building the data model, to solving the time intelligence limitation.
