## Day 11 – Guided Excel Project (Completed)

![](https://github.com/omolola-A/120-days-data-analytics-journey/blob/main/Day10_Img1.png)

Wrapped up the guided Excel project today.

I added a geographical map chart, built a column chart for the top five states by revenue, and created a segment distribution by region. After that, I pulled the summary insights from the analysis sheet into a dedicated “Summary” section on the dashboard.

I also inserted and formatted text boxes to act as navigation links between the Dashboard, Analysis, and Dataset sheets. Added slicers and cleaned up their formatting to make the report more interactive.

What stood out to me in this project wasn’t just the calculations, but the structure — how modeling, analysis, and presentation all connect. Small layout decisions made a big difference in clarity.

Looking back at my earlier Excel work, this feels more intentional. Next step is rebuilding something similar without guidance and seeing what gaps show up.
