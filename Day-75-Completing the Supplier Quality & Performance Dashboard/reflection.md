### Completing the Supplier Quality & Performance Dashboard

![](https://github.com/omolola-A/120-days-data-analytics-journey/blob/main/Day%2073a.png)

I finally completed the Supplier Quality and Performance Dashboard after days of building, adjusting, and learning through the process.

The project covers overview reporting, vendor performance, plant analysis, materials, and downtime impact. Beyond the analysis itself, a big part of the learning came from the dashboard experience and interaction design.

I already knew basic page navigation in Power BI, but this project pushed it further. I worked with bookmarks, the selection pane, hidden slicers, interactive navigation, and visibility controls that make elements appear or disappear with clicks. Small details, but they changed how the dashboard feels and flows.

There were moments where things broke or didn’t behave as expected, especially with interactions and layered visuals, so a lot of today was testing, adjusting, and trying again.

This project reminded me that building dashboards is not just about visuals or measures—it’s also about creating an experience that makes the insights easier to explore.
