## Day 10 – Guided Excel Project (Dashboard Build)

![](https://github.com/omolola-A/120-days-data-analytics-journey/blob/main/Day10_Img.png)


Still working through the Excel project, and today was fully focused on the dashboard.

I referenced the KPIs from the analysis sheet into the dashboard, formatted the values properly, and built charts for revenue, quantity, orders, and discounts. A lot of small adjustments went into spacing, alignment, and number formatting — the kind of details that don’t look big but make a difference.

One thing that stood out was using the Camera tool to link the time-intelligence KPIs directly onto the dashboard. It was my first time trying it, and it changed how I think about presenting dynamic elements in Excel.

I also created monthly trend charts, a doughnut chart for revenue by customer segment (and learned how to resize individual slices), and a revenue-by-product-category chart.

What I’m noticing is that building the dashboard takes as much thinking as the analysis itself. Getting the numbers right is one step. Arranging them so they make sense at a glance is another.

Still refining my eye for layout and clarity.
