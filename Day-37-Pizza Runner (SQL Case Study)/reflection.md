## Day 37/120 – Pizza Runner (SQL Case Study)

![](https://github.com/omolola-A/120-days-data-analytics-journey/blob/main/Day_37.png)

Today I continued working with SQL using the Pizza Runner case study.

This one came with a lot more questions, grouped into different areas. I focused on the Pizza Metrics section, answering questions around orders, customers, deliveries, and timing patterns.

The queries themselves weren’t always difficult individually, but writing them back-to-back exposed a few gaps. I had to be more careful with filtering for successful deliveries, counting correctly without duplication, and breaking down orders by time (hour and day).

One thing I noticed is how quickly simple questions become tricky when the data isn’t perfectly clean or when multiple conditions are involved. I had to slow down and think through each step instead of rushing to write queries.

This felt like less about syntax and more about being precise with logic.
