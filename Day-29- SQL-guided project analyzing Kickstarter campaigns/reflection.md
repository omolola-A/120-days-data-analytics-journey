## Day 29/120 of my Data Analytics Challenge.

![](https://github.com/omolola-A/120-days-data-analytics-journey/blob/main/Day29.png)

I dove into a SQL-guided project analyzing Kickstarter campaigns to spot patterns in failed projects. I explored the database schema, selected relevant columns, filtered data with WHERE, sorted with ORDER BY, and applied conditional logic using CASE.

One key observation: many campaigns labeled “failed” had actually reached 75–87% of their funding goal. They attracted strong support but didn’t hit 100%, which is why they officially failed. It’s a reminder that data labels don’t always tell the full story.
