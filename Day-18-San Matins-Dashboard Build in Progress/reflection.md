## Day 18 – Dashboard Build in Progress

![](https://github.com/omolola-A/120-days-data-analytics-journey/blob/main/Day18_IMG.png)

Today was about visual structure.

I added formatted charts to the San Martin Stores dashboard and inserted slicers to prepare for interactivity. They’re not fully connected yet — just placed, styled, and aligned.

It doesn’t look impressive at this stage. Some charts still need adjustments. The slicers don’t control everything yet. But the layout is starting to feel intentional.

I’m learning that building dashboards is rarely linear. You design, adjust, reconnect, reformat… then repeat.

Next step is wiring everything properly and testing the interactions to make sure the story actually flows.
