# Day 1 of my 120-day data analytics build.
Today, I stepped back from building and reviewed three entry-level Data Analyst job descriptions to check how my current skills line up with what’s actually being asked for. Excel, Power BI, and SQL showed up across all three, especially around cleaning data, building summaries, and communicating insights clearly. One role mentioned VBA and data mining, which reminded me there’s still a gap between what I can already do comfortably and what I haven’t applied yet.
What stood out most wasn’t a new tool. It was the emphasis on thinking through problems and explaining results in a way stakeholders can act on. That part feels less like a checklist item and more like something, I have to keep practicing with every project I build.
I’m currently working at an early-career level using Excel for cleaning and quick analysis, SQL for querying and aggregations, Power BI for dashboards, and Python for loading and manipulating data. The experience requirement (1–2 years) still shows up often, which is a reminder that these portfolio projects are not optional—they are my experience right now.
Next step: Start intentionally building at least one project that pushes me slightly beyond my comfort zone, possibly automating part of my workflow or exploring deeper analysis than simple summaries.
