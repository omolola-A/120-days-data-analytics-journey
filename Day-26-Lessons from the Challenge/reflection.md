## Day 26/120 – Lessons from the Challenge

Reflecting on my 120-day Data Analytics Challenge, today I focused on the bigger picture: what this journey is really teaching me.

Key takeaways so far:

* Discipline – Showing up consistently, building daily habits.

* Learning publicly – Being transparent about progress, mistakes, and insights.

* Building projects – Turning messy datasets into meaningful dashboards and analysis.

* Facing real challenges – Navigating incomplete data, ambiguous questions, and unexpected patterns.

This challenge isn’t just about tools like Excel, SQL, or Power BI. It’s about learning how to think like a data analyst: reasoning, problem-solving, and communicating insights effectively.

Next step: Refine projects into a portfolio that demonstrates skills, problem-solving, and growth over the 120 days.
