## Day 8 – Guided Excel Project: Sales Overview Analysis

![](https://github.com/omolola-A/120-days-data-analytics-journey/blob/main/Screenshot%202026-02-19%20120223.png)

Today was hands-on.

I worked through a guided Excel project focused on building a proper sales overview from raw data. The first step was cleaning the dataset — removing irrelevant fields, checking for inconsistencies, and making sure the structure made sense before doing anything else. That part alone reminded me how easy it is to rush into analysis without preparing the data properly.

After cleaning, I converted the dataset into a table and pushed it into a Pivot Table to start exploring patterns. From there, I calculated key KPIs: Total Revenue, Profit Generated, Total Orders, Average Discount, and Total Quantity Sold.

The part that stretched me was building a Date Table to enable time-based analysis. Creating relationships between tables and writing measures like Revenue YoY, Quantity YoY, Orders YoY, Discount YoY, Revenue PY, and a Location Summary forced me to slow down and think about how the data model connects behind the scenes.

Formatting the values and building KPI cards (Revenue, Orders, Quantity, Discount) made everything feel more structured and business-focused rather than just numbers on a sheet.

What stood out most is how much can actually be done in Excel when it’s used intentionally. I used to see it as just a spreadsheet tool. Now I’m starting to see the modeling side of it.

Next, I want to rebuild this without following the guide step-by-step and see what I truly understand on my own.##
